# 📊 Customer Demographic Analysis | Tableau Dashboard

### Turning Customer Data into Meaningful Business Insights

An interactive Tableau dashboard developed to analyse **5,000 customer records** and understand the relationship between customer demographics and purchasing behaviour. This project explores customer segments, sales performance, product preferences, and monthly sales trends to identify potential business opportunities and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customers is essential for businesses to improve their sales strategies, customer engagement, and marketing performance.

This project combines customer demographic information with purchasing data to explore:

- Who are the major customer segments?
- Which age groups contribute more to sales?
- How do income levels influence purchasing patterns?
- Which product categories perform better?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- Where are customers geographically distributed?

The analysis is presented through an interactive Tableau dashboard with KPIs, visualizations, and filters.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business has customer demographic and purchasing data, but analysing these datasets separately makes it difficult to identify valuable customer segments, understand purchasing patterns, and recognize sales opportunities.

**The objective is to transform raw customer data into meaningful insights that can support better marketing strategies and business decisions.**

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Analyse customer distribution across demographic segments.
2. Identify age groups contributing the highest sales.
3. Evaluate sales performance across different income levels.
4. Identify top-performing product categories.
5. Examine geographical customer distribution.
6. Analyse monthly sales trends.
7. Provide actionable recommendations based on the findings.

---

## 📂 Dataset Details

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Dataset Source | Kaggle – Store Sales Dataset |
| Total Records | 5,000 |
| Total Attributes | 21 |
| File Format | CSV |
| Analysis Tool | Tableau |

**Key attributes include:**

- CustomerID
- OrderID
- Age and AgeGroup
- Gender
- IncomeBracket
- Occupation
- City, State and Region
- Category and ItemPurchased
- Amount and AverageOrderValue
- PurchaseFrequency
- PurchaseDate and Season
- PaymentMethod
- ItemRating
- DiscountApplied (%)
- PreviousPurchases

*Note: The project dataset was enhanced for the analysis with additional demographic and derived fields. Some fields represent project-defined or synthetic values rather than independently verified customer information.*

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes five KPIs to provide an overview of customer and sales performance.

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | Number of customer records analysed |
| Total Sales | Sum of transaction amounts |
| Total Orders | Number of order records |
| Average Order Value | Average transaction amount |
| Average Purchase Frequency | Average value of the PurchaseFrequency field |

---

## 📉 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains six major visualizations:

**1. Customer Distribution by Age Group**

Analyses the distribution of customers across different age segments.

**2. Sales by Age Group**

Identifies which age groups contribute the highest sales.

**3. Sales by Product Category**

Compares sales performance across product categories using a treemap.

**4. Sales by Income Level**

Examines sales contributions across different customer income brackets.

**5. State-wise Customer Distribution**

Uses a geographical map to explore customer distribution across Indian states.

**6. Monthly Sales Trend**

Tracks monthly sales performance and highlights variations over time.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **45–54 age group:** Generated the highest sales contribution.
- **35–44 age group:** Also demonstrated strong sales performance.
- **Middle-income customers:** Contributed the largest share of sales.
- **Electronics:** Emerged as the highest-selling product category.
- **Gender distribution:** Male and female customer representation was relatively balanced.
- **Monthly sales:** August recorded the highest sales, while February recorded the lowest among the months analysed.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the insights identified:

1. **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing campaigns on the 35–54 age segments.
2. **Customer Loyalty:** Introduce loyalty offers and personalized promotions to encourage repeat purchases.
3. **Product Strategy:** Explore cross-selling and promotional opportunities within the Electronics category.
4. **Customer Engagement:** Develop campaigns to improve engagement among younger customer segments.
5. **Regional Opportunities:** Explore potential growth opportunities in states with lower customer representation.
6. **Sales Planning:** Use monthly sales trends to support promotional and inventory planning.
